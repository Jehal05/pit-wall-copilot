# Notice

## Data and names
Pit Wall Copilot is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Formula 1, the FIA, or any team, driver or circuit.

- Team names, driver names and circuit layouts in the web dashboard are used only so the demo is recognisable. No official logos, fonts or imagery are used.
- Every number is simulated and illustrative: lap times, tyre and fuel models, weather, results and standings. Nothing is real telemetry.
- The original backend demo scenario (`scenarios/scenario-demo-01.json`) and the sporting-rule excerpts under `docs/sporting-rules/` are fictional and written for this project.

## Third-party data
Circuit outlines are simplified from the open [f1-circuits](https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits) data, derived from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## Status
The code runs locally. Nothing has been deployed to or validated on AWS.
