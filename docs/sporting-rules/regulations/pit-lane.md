# Pit Lane Procedure and Pit Loss

Regulation-Id: reg-pitlane-001
Version: 1.0

## Pit loss components

Fact-Id: reg-pitlane-001-components
Keywords: pit loss, pit entry, lane transit, stationary time, pit exit, four components, pit lane speed, pit-lane speed, lane speed, speed limit, pit limit, mandatory pit limit, pit entry speed

Pit loss is made up of four components, each modeled separately and added
together exactly once, on the lap the stop occurs: pit entry (the
reduced-speed transit into the pit lane), lane transit (traveling the
length of the pit lane at the mandatory speed limit), stationary time (the
time physically stopped at the pit box), and pit exit (the transit back
onto the racing line). There is no fifth component and no separate penalty
added beyond these four.

## Tire warm-up loss on the out-lap

Fact-Id: reg-pitlane-001-warmup
Keywords: warm-up loss, in-lap, out-lap, cold tires, working temperature

A driver who has just left the pits is on tires that have not yet reached
their working temperature. The out-lap (and, to a lesser extent, the lap
after it) is measurably slower than subsequent laps on the same set, an
effect referred to as tire warm-up loss. This loss applies once per pit
stop, immediately after the stop, and does not recur later in the stint.
